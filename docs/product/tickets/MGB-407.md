# MGB-407 — Settlement cutoff regression

**Type:** Bug
**Priority:** Critical
**Owner:** Payments Platform

## Problem

A change under review mis-classifies USD wire payments around the New York cutoff when the United States is on daylight saving time.

## Scope

The defect is **not on `main`**. It belongs on `demo/mgb-407-cutoff-regression` only.

On 18 March 2026, 16:30 in New York is 20:30 UTC (EDT, UTC−4), not 21:30 UTC. A wire submitted at 20:45 UTC must roll to the 19 March window. A wire submitted at 18:40 UTC is still inside the 18 March window, 110 minutes from cutoff.

The January control case is unchanged: 16:30 New York is 21:30 UTC while the US is on standard time.

Reproduction, the expected results, and the observed buggy results are in the presenter kit, not in this repository.
